Identifiers are removed before analysis

Before your document text is sent for analysis, we replace personal identifiers with placeholders: South African ID numbers, email addresses, telephone numbers and account numbers. The real values are put back only in the review shown on your screen and in the copy emailed to you.

We want to be straight with you about the limit of this. It removes identifiers. It does not remove names, which cannot be detected reliably by pattern alone, and a name in a contract is still personal information. So this reduces what crosses the border. It does not reduce it to nothing.

Analysis is performed by [[AI_PROVIDER]], located in [[AI_PROVIDER_COUNTRY]]. This is a transfer of personal information outside the Republic, governed by section 72 of POPIA.

If you would prefer that your document is not processed outside South Africa, do not upload it, and contact us instead.

Security

We take the steps section 19 of POPIA requires: encrypted connections, restricted access to the order records, no storage of uploaded documents, and deletion of review text after the retention period. If personal information is ever accessed by someone unauthorised, we will notify the Information Regulator and you, as section 22 requires.
